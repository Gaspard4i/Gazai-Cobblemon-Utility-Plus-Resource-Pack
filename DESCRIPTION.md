# Gazai's Cobblemon Utility+ Resource Pack

New names and sprites for the items of [Cobblemon Utility+](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-utility-plus), the same ones as in my mod [Cobblemon Utility Items](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-utility-items). It needs Cobblemon Utility+ 1.8.0 (Minecraft 1.21.1) and changes nothing else.

All 38 items are covered: the stat candies, the Shining ones, the Moldy and Rotten ones (the old Obsidian and Void candies), Mystery Candy, Outdated Rare Candy, Moldy Nutrient, Moldy Poke Snack, Apricorn Energy Drink, Master Aprijuice, a new Ball Synchronizer and the rest.

Languages: English, French, Spanish, German and Italian. The Spanish, German and Italian files use the English names for the renamed items.

## Credits

Almost all the sprites are Cobblemon's own sprites, recolored or edited, so they are not really mine. Only two or three are not from Cobblemon, and those are mine.
